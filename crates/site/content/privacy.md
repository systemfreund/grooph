grooph is a metronome and rhythm trainer that runs in your browser at grooph.app and as an
Android app. It works without an account, shows no ads and contains no analytics or
tracking code. This page explains which data is processed anyway and why.

Last updated: 18 October 2026

## Data stored on your device

grooph remembers your settings, tempo, saved presets, rhythms and practice results so they
are still there the next time you open it. In the browser this data is kept in the local
storage of your browser; in the Android app it is kept in a file in the app's private
storage. It never leaves your device and is not sent to us. You can delete it by clearing
the site data in your browser or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

## Microphone

If you turn on the microphone for timing practice, grooph listens to the audio signal to
detect when you hit a drum or practice pad. The audio is analysed on your device as it
arrives and is neither recorded, stored nor transmitted. The browser or Android asks for
your permission before the microphone is used (on Android this is the only permission the
app requests), and you can withdraw it at any time in the browser or system settings.

## MIDI devices

If you connect a MIDI device, grooph reads its note messages on your device to show your
timing. Nothing is stored or transmitted.

## Sharing rhythms

When you share a rhythm, grooph creates a link that contains the tempo and the rhythm
itself, for example `grooph.app/?v=2&bpm=90&r=…`. The link contains no personal data. It is
passed only to the app or contact you choose, such as a messenger, and opening it simply
loads the rhythm in grooph.

## Hosting and server logs

The website and web app are hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. (Cloudflare Pages). When you open
grooph.app, your browser sends a request to Cloudflare's servers, which necessarily
processes your IP address and technical request data such as the time, the requested page
and your browser's user agent. Cloudflare uses this data to deliver the site and protect it
against attacks and may store it in server logs for a short time. The legal basis is our
legitimate interest in a secure and working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Cloudflare may
process data in the USA; it is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See
[Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

While the web app is open, it checks about once an hour whether a new version has been
published by loading a small version file from grooph.app. This request is handled like
any other page request and sends no data beyond the technical request data above.

The Android app does not connect to our servers. It is distributed through Google Play;
Google's [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies to downloading and
updating the app there.

## Children

grooph does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of
processing, data portability and to object to processing of your personal data, as well
as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Since
grooph does not store personal data about you on our side, there is usually nothing for us
to access or delete; for any questions, write to the email address above.

## Changes

If grooph starts processing data in a new way, this page will be updated first and the
date above changed.
