Every rhythm becomes easier to read once you can **count** it. These guides explain the
counting syllables musicians use and show each figure with an example you can play in
grooph. Every example has a **Play** link that opens it with a metronome.

```pages
Learn
```

## The counting syllables at a glance

| Note value | Per beat | Counted |
|---|---|---|
| Quarter notes | 1 | 1 2 3 4 |
| Eighth notes | 2 | 1 & 2 & |
| Eighth-note triplets | 3 | 1 trip let 2 trip let |
| Sixteenth notes | 4 | 1 e & a 2 e & a |

```rhythm bpm=60 | All four in one measure
4/4 q e e t8 t8 t8 s s s s
```

When you are ready to read new material, the [sight-reading exercises](/sight-reading/)
generate random rhythms at your level.
